About

More about me

A part designer, part engineer. I’ve been building things for a while now — sometimes that means designing UIs, other times it’s writing Python backends, wiring up automations, or figuring out how a product should actually work.

I like working on real problems with people who care. I’ve collaborated with early-stage startups, nonprofits, and tech communities to help them design better systems, ship faster, and make things a little more human.

Outside of work, you’ll probably find me hosting Python meetups, reading and writing about psychology & philosophy, and playing with dogs :)

Behind the Work

  • 01.

    Understand & Ask

    I start by understanding the product, the people it’s for, and the goals you’re trying to hit. This usually means asking lots of questions and digging into how things currently work (or don’t).

  • 02.

    Explore & Sketch

    Once I have context, I start mapping out ideas — flows, wireframes, maybe a few experiments in Figma or code. The goal is to figure out what makes sense before making it pretty.

  • 03.

    Design & Build

    Now comes the real work. I design clean, useful interfaces and/or build the backend and frontend to bring them to life. Everything stays connected to the original goals.

  • 04.

    Test, Improve, Repeat

    I ship early and improve fast. I’ll review feedback, fix rough edges, and help you keep things evolving — whether it’s a UI tweak or a system-level refactor.
